First — what Nave is

Your content department, in a terminal.

What it is

A Content OS you install and own

Nave runs on Claude Code, on your machine. Not SaaS, not a subscription to software — a working system of agents covering every stage of production, from research to publishing. It installs per brand, with your voice and rules written in.

What it's for

One production day, one month of content

You bring footage and direction. The system carries pre-production, production, post-production, distribution and engagement — and stages everything for your approval. Nothing posts without your OK.

How you use it

Plain English. No code.

You direct it the way you'd direct a team: in plain English, in a terminal. The training inside walks the install step by step, the live calls fix real screens, and support answers the same day.

Nave lives only inside the community — the OS is never sold or downloaded anywhere else.
