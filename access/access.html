Do I have to use the AI Workspace template?+

No. If you already have a workspace, Nave can live in its own content department folder there. Open that installed folder in your agent. The AI Workspace template is an optional starting point for organizing a new workspace.

Do I need to fork the repositories?+

No. For a new AI Workspace, create an independent private repository from the template. For Nave, keep its source checkout separate and use the installation guide. If you already have a fork with work in it, preserve it and ask for help with the existing-installation route.

What happens to my existing files?+

Keep them where they are. Choose a new department folder or use the documented existing-installation route after a backup and plan review. Don't replace your workspace's instruction files or install over existing content blindly.

How do I get updates?+

Review updates in the separate Nave source checkout, back up your work, and follow the upgrade guide. Updates should preserve your brand and content records. An independent AI Workspace copy does not automatically sync with its template.
