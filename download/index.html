Windows says "Windows protected your PC"+

Nave is new and not yet signed with a Windows certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway. You only do this once.

My Mac says Nave is damaged or can't be opened+

Nave isn't signed by Apple yet, and macOS blocks apps it can't verify. Drag Nave into Applications, open Terminal, paste xattr -cr /Applications/Nave.app and press Return, then open Nave. If it then asks, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

I have an Intel Mac+

Nave runs on Apple silicon Macs (M1 and newer) for now. Ask in the community and we'll tell you when an Intel version is ready.

What stays on my computer?+

Your workspace, your brand records and your content stay in your own folder. Your tool keys stay on your computer; Nave never holds them.
